A genealogy of the exemplarist tradition

The Living River & its channels

One current runs from Plato to the present: the world as the expression of a divine Mind, and the soul's ascent back toward it. It rises in Athens, is baptized by the Fathers, matured in the cloister, lost and recovered, then secularized and inverted in the modern age. Every figure here was named in the making of this map. Hover any name to see where it stands in the flow.

Drag to scroll. The wide gap after the Fathers is real — the Latin West kept only half a Timaeus for eight hundred years.

A living map — nodes sit by lifespan, colored by the current they belong to; the channels interrelate by inheritance and by reaction, not as a war. It pairs with the rhizomal map from the sister thread, and it's built to grow: add a figure, recolor a channel, publish it when it's ready. The order of knowing is the order of return.
